SnapLite

SnapLite needs permission to capture the current tab. Firefox only allows screenshot capture with <all_urls> access or a granted activeTab, and it cannot show that prompt inside the in-page panel — which is why this tab opened.

  • Click Allow screen capture below.
  • Choose Allow in Firefox's prompt (it asks for “Access your data for all websites”).
  • This tab closes; go back to your page and press Capture & Answer.
